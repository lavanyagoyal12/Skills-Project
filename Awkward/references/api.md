Read this when you need to confirm a function exists, its exact signature, or find the right `ak.*` operation by task.

Verified against `awkward` 2.14.0. Signatures are from `inspect.signature`; decorator-added `highlevel=True, behavior=None, attrs=None` tails are omitted. Deeper notes: `high-level.md` (creating), `types-layouts.md` (type/validity), `backends-behaviors.md` (backend/Numba/JAX), `advanced-patterns.md` (examples and gotchas). Notation: `?T` option, `var * T` variable list, `N * T` regular list.
